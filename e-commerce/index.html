FAQs

Common e-commerce questions

Can I take payments through PBI?

Yes. PBI uses Stripe payment routes for online checkout and can support product-led websites and small stores.

Can I use PBI outside the UK?

Yes. The site can be positioned for international customers. Stripe availability, tax, shipping and legal wording should be checked for the countries you sell in.

Can PBI build the store for me?

Yes. You can use the builder yourself or request assisted setup or a custom build when you want PBI to handle more of the structure, copy, design and launch checks.
