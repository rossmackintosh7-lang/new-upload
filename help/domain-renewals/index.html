Domains

Choose the web address your customers will use.

A domain is the website address people type into their browser. PBI gives you three simple choices when you publish.

PBI web address

Fastest option. Your site goes live on a PBI address and you can add a custom domain later.

Connect a domain you own

Use this if you already bought a domain elsewhere. PBI will show you the DNS steps needed to point it at your website.

Register a new domain

Search for an available domain, save your choice, and PBI can add the domain cost at checkout when supported.
