Payments

Choose how customers should pay, book or enquire.

Not every website needs online checkout. PBI helps you choose the right route for your business before publishing.

Enquiry first

Best for services, quotes and custom work. Customers send details and you reply before payment.

Booking link

Best if you already use a calendar or booking tool. Add the booking link to your buttons and contact page.

Checkout or shop

Best for products, deposits or fixed-price services. Ask PBI if you need help setting up products and payment flow.
