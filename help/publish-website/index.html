Publishing

Make your website live when it is ready.

You can build, edit and preview for free. Publishing is the point where you choose to make the website public and start the paid service.

1

Save your latest changes

Use the save button in the builder so your latest wording, images, pages and domain choice are stored.

2

Run the checklist

Check your business name, main offer, contact route, mobile view, images, spelling and SEO basics before publishing.

3

Choose your domain route

Use a PBI web address, connect a domain you already own, or choose a new domain to register during checkout.

4

Complete checkout

PBI sends you to checkout for the package and any selected add-ons. Your website goes live after payment is complete.
