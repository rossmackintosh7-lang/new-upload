What PBI checks

A useful review, not a generic score report.

The audit focuses on what a real customer sees and what Google needs to understand. You get practical notes that can turn into a self-build improvement plan, Website Management, SEO Care or a done-for-you build.

Search visibility

Titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, schema, content depth and whether the page targets the right search intent.

Conversion flow

Offer clarity, trust proof, calls to action, enquiry routes, checkout friction and whether the next step is obvious.

Mobile experience

Spacing, readable text, button size, sticky elements, menus, forms and whether the site feels usable on a phone.

Technical basics

Speed signals, image size, broken links, canonical tags, accessibility basics and obvious launch risks.

Common questions

Before you request an audit.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The audit is a practical first review. If you want PBI to make changes afterwards, PBI will recommend the right route and confirm any cost before work starts.

What does PBI check?

PBI checks the offer, page structure, mobile layout, SEO basics, headings, metadata, speed, image use, trust signals, calls to action, forms and likely next improvements.

Can this turn into a custom build?

Yes. If the website needs deeper work, PBI can recommend a Basic, Standard, Premium, E-commerce or Complex build route.
